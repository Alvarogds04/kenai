# kenai-0.2
Kenai 0.2: Kenai and Koda as two independent applications that can connect to share chats, automations and mini-Kodas. Base: `release/0.1` at `9d25455`. Kenai 0.1 is not published yet; nothing here ships in it.
The vision is large and not yet cut into slices: the first slice is decided with the human (T1).

## Specs
- S1: Two applications, each working on its own: Kenai on the phone, Koda on the computer. They can connect to each other, for example to share chat information, automations and mini-Kodas, «pero cada uno trabaja a su forma y a su manera». Neither depends on the other. (Replaces the first reading of «2a», chat shared only through the link. It also answers the open question: the phone chats on its own.)
- S2: Other apps (Tasker, Automate, Samsung Routines) can react to Kenai and trigger it through Android intents and broadcasts («3a»). Kept; it is the smallest piece of phone-side automation.
- S3: The user automates from the computer and from the phone. The example Koda already has, the Telegram channel check about «mis acciones», must also be something the user can set up with Kenai: «Y también Kenai me lo tiene que permitir». (Replaces the first reading of «3c», scheduled tasks run only by Koda.)
- S4: Decided («2a», correcting an earlier «2b»). An automation never allows a permission request by itself: Allow always asks for the fingerprint or the screen lock, as in 0.1. An Android intent from another app can never carry an allow either.
- S5: Decided («1a»). Kenai uses the internet on its own for its chat and automations (AI providers, Telegram) and keeps its secrets, the provider keys and the Telegram bot token, in the Android Keystore. Replaces «LAN only, no relay». Proposed, to confirm: what Kenai sends out is limited to what the user configured, and to the provider or channel he configured.
- S8: Decided. The chat providers are the local model through LiteLLM, plus Claude, GPT and «nan builders» (not yet identified: see L10). Several providers from the start, not one.
- S9: Decided. The Mini-Koda engine already exists in Koda, so Kenai uses it as the backend: all the flows run on the machine at the human's home, and Kenai drives them through the link. Kenai does not get its own Mini-Koda engine. (Recommendation 2 of the plan, accepted.)
- S10: Decided. Kenai and Koda must be downloadable and installable by other users on their own machines, «si lo desean». Consequences for the design: nothing hard-coded to the human's machine (no LiteLLM address, no Tailscale name, no key); every endpoint is a setting; the app works on its own without Koda; a release APK on GitHub with install instructions.
- S11: Decided. No hurry for 0.1: the Kenai 0.2 work can start now, in parallel with the 0.1 test on the real phone.
- S6: A central system, «un Jarvis de lo que sería Eineman»: the user sends things to it and it answers «en un lenguaje natural y neutral».
- S12: Decided (L20). One standard engine for both apps: «es mejor crear un motor para ambos que sea estandar tanto para kenai y koda, ya que asi ellos siempre se conectan a ese motor que tiene montado el usuario y pueden existir todo tipo de dispositivo». It runs where the user puts it: «el motor y toda la carga pesada de kenai tiene que correr o en un VPS o una pc local en donde podamos de ahi conectar con el telefono y poder tener mis chats, mis automatizaciones y todo mi espacio personal para ello». Kenai, Koda and any other device are clients of it. This reshapes S1 (the apps no longer each carry their own engine) and S9 (the engine is no longer Koda's).
- S13: Decided (L19-L20). Not LAN only: «no quiero una dependencia que se concte o se enlace solo por la red local». Tunnels are not the user's solution («3c entendedamos que seto no es una solucion para mi»). Chosen «2a+b»: A, a relay with end-to-end encryption that the engine dials out to (no open ports, no account, works behind a home router), plus B, a direct TLS connection when the engine has a public address (a VPS); direct on the LAN when both are on it; pairing by QR. Open: who runs the relay (the user on his VPS, or a hosted service with a cost); the relay waits until after the chat (L22).
- S14: Decided (L19). The chat goes through the engine's Gentle Shell and all its providers: «puedes hacer un brigde que ya existen y lo usamos ya que el motor sera Gentle shell y asi usamos todos sus proveedores que ya tiene gentle dots». Replaces the Claude adapter in Kenai (T1a-4); the direct providers on the phone (T1a-1..3b, S5, S8) become secondary or are archived.
- S15: Decided (L20, «3 si por favor»). First slice, user stories HU1, HU2, HU3, HU6:
  - HU1: «Instalo el motor en mi PC o VPS con un solo paso y me muestra un QR.»
  - HU2: «Escaneo el QR y quedo conectado, sin abrir puertos, sin crear cuentas y sin túneles.»
  - HU3: «Con datos móviles chateo con cualquier modelo que tenga Gentle Shell, y el historial vive en el host.»
  - HU6: «Si el host está apagado, Kenai me lo dice claro y me deja leer lo que ya tenía.»
  - Later: HU4 automations and notifications, HU5 personal space (memory, notes, files) on the host, HU7 approvals from the phone, HU8 revoking a lost phone.
- S16: Decided (L19). «de momento podemos no subir hasta que el chat se pueda usar»: Kenai 0.1 is not published until the chat is usable; then 2a (fast-forward main, tag v0.1.0, no PR).
- S17: Decided (L22, «1a, 2b»). The engine lives in a new repository of its own (not inside Koda's repo), built from Koda's daemon and runtime (L21). It holds several conversations with a list, not one continuous chat shared by every device; this changes the engine (today `new_conversation` is refused).
- S7: Mini-Kodas: specialised AI agents for specific processes. «Si un proceso no requiere una IA, pues que tenga su Chrome Job con sus scripts». A process that needs no AI is a cron job with its own scripts.

- L21: T5 map (read-only explorer over Koda worktree release-0.2; nothing run). Koda is three layers: the Tauri island (only host and renderer), the Node daemon `apps/desktop-daemon` (supervises the engine, accounts and profiles, history projection, uploads, Mini-Kodas, «Hoy», private Engram), and the engine Gentle Shell spawned as `gentle-shell --mode rpc` (Pi RPC over stdin/stdout). The daemon and the engine already run without a GUI (`koda-daemon run`); the bundled runtime (Node 22, Pi 1.4.0, gentle-pi 4.3.0, Engram 3.4.0) comes from `packaging/runtime/build-runtime.cjs`. Clients talk to the daemon over a local socket `island-agent/1` (JSONL, per-launch token, 4 client slots); there is NO TCP listener. Chat history already lives on the host (engine session files under `<island>/sessions/`), one continuous chat per daemon, `new_conversation` refused. Providers are per-account `models.json` + private `auth.json` (0600), file-based and headless-capable; keys never return to clients.
