# kenai-0.1
Release 0.1 of Kenai for Android, on branch `release/0.1`: a functional canvas replica of today's app, plus the group C features (light theme, Spanish, vibration, quick settings tile actions).
Base: `beb41a8`. Previous plans: `odd/tasks/abito-movil-base.md`, `odd/tasks/diseno-movil-isla.md` (history).

## Specs
- S1: «quiero una maqueta de canvas que sea funcional igual a la app de verdad» + «1a» → the replica reproduces the real app as it is today (English texts, current look), not the «Isla en el bolsillo» direction. Every real screen is reachable (Pairing, Scan, Home, Chat, Settings, History, Gallery, Diagnostics, Licences, Session details), the bottom bar and buttons navigate, the approval/question/diff sheets work (Allow/Deny/Answer, simulated fingerprint), the 9 widgets are shown on an Android home screen and follow the demo's 8-step loop, and fake data fills the features the desktop does not serve yet (chat, question options, diffs, usage, services, session details).
- S2: «metemos todo de la C» → group C goes into release 0.1:
  - S2.5 «Tema claro»: the app gets a light theme (today it is dark only). Decided (L5, option 1a): it follows the system's dark/light mode, no new setting; the island, the sheets, the scan screen and the Koda hero card stay dark in both.
  - S2.6 «Español en la app»: the app is available in Spanish; this replaces the repo's «English only» rule. Decided (L5, option 2a): Spanish when the phone is in Spanish, English otherwise; no per-app picker. English stays the default.
  - S2.7a «Vibración al llegar una aprobación o una pregunta». Decided (L5, option 3a): a new "Vibrate" switch, on by default; it vibrates during quiet hours too (approvals and questions always notify); no double buzz when the approval notification already vibrates.
  - S2.7b «acciones en la tile de ajustes rápidos, que hoy solo abre la app». Decided (L5, options 4a+4b): the tile subtitle shows what waits ("N waiting" or the most urgent agent), and a tap opens the pending approval or question directly (Home when nothing waits). No Allow/Deny from the tile, no quiet-hours toggle.
- S4: From the emulator check (L12): switching the phone between light and dark sends the app back to Home (seen from Settings); the open screen must stay.
- S5: «veo que el diseño tiene referencias visuales de abito cambiado eso por koda en la app» → every place the app draws Abito's green clay character (sheets, pairing, gallery, island, widgets and their previews, launcher icon) shows Koda instead. Wire IDs (`abito://pair`, `_abito._tcp`) stay until K2-12.
- S6: «todo en espaól» → on a Spanish phone everything is Spanish: the Koda desktop menus quoted in Spanish stay; the demo's status texts and the copied diagnostics report are Spanish too.
- S7: «si arreglalo» → the bottom bar must not cover the buttons of the Pairing "Paste the link" sheet and the Home address sheet.
- S8: «podemos tambien ver el diseño, cambia las mascotas todo por koda y mejoras la UI UX CX? hazme 3 diseños de mejoras» → (a) the canvas replica draws Koda wherever it still draws the clay mascot (sheets, pairing, chat, session, gallery, widgets); (b) a new design `design/kenai-mejoras/` with 3 clearly different improvement directions for UI, UX and CX, Koda everywhere, Spanish, tokens of design/system.md.
- S9: Handoff «Implement the design artboard design/kenai-mejoras/01-decidi-en-un-toque.html» (title «1 · Decidí en un toque»; tradeoff «Lo más rápido y seguro para decidir (un toque y la huella desde Inicio, con resultado confirmado); con un pedido abierto Inicio deja de mostrar a Koda grande y el progreso de los demás»). The artboard is the visual spec; map its parts to the app's components, tokens from design/system.md, never copy its HTML. Screens: 1 Inicio with the pending request as the hero (agent, action, exact command, risk line, expiry, Denegar / Permitir with fingerprint), island-style status strip, «También te espera», «En curso», badge on Inicio; 2 confirm with fingerprint (the system prompt); 3 result states enviando / permitido / denegado / caducó / no se pudo enviar with «Lo siguiente»; 4 question with progress and option descriptions; 5 notification with Revisar el pedido and the «Aprobaciones pendientes» widget. Haptics: one buzz allow, two deny. Inicio states: con pedido, «Nada te espera», sin conexión with «Sin confirmar».
- S3: No Coucou name, Mochi, icon or sounds (NOTICE.md). Nothing on the desktop side is touched in this repo.
